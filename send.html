      Send straight from Apple Maps (optional one-time setup)
      1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name it Send to Waypoint.
      2. Tap the i (or the settings icon), turn on Show in Share Sheet, and set it to receive Locations.
      3. Add Get Details of Locations → Latitude (from Shortcut Input). Add it again for Longitude, and again for Name.
      4. Add URL Encode on the Name result.
      5. Add Open URLs with this text, dropping in the three results where shown:
      6. In Apple Maps, tap a place → Share → Send to Waypoint, then tap Send.
